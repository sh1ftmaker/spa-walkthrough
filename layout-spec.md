# Kazan-yu layout spec (source of truth for plans and 3D)

The isometric cutaway (`images/24-isometric-cutaway.png`) settles any conflict between images. This file pins down the numbers.

## Orientation and grid
- Plan view: north is up. West is on the left and east is on the right, the same as the cutaway. The sea is to the east.
- Shared grid, 200 m × 120 m: columns A–J (20 m each, west to east) and rows 1–6 (20 m each, north to south).
- Every level plan prints this grid so the plans stack when modelled.

## Vertical connections (same grid cell on every level)
Every place can be reached on foot. Lifts and the slide are extras.
- **E3, main lift and Spiral Stair.** A lift in the middle of a carved spiral staircase, running from Ground through B1, B2, B3 and B4.
- **Terrace Path.** A switchback footpath and stair climbing the south face beside the Cascade Terraces, from the Ground at B5 to the Summit at E3. The cable lift (E4) runs parallel.
- **Cliff Stair.** An outdoor stair cut into the north cliff, joining Ground (G3), each suite landing and the Summit moon deck (H2).
- **Mist Steps, B1 → B2 at C3.** A wide stepped reflecting-pool stair, mist-covered, that descends to the Kawagiri entrance.
- **Falls Stair, B2 → B3 at B3–B4.** A stair carved into the cliff that passes behind the Thunder Curtain Falls on a walkway.
- **Lava Stair, B1 → B3 at H4.** A stair that winds around the Magma Glass Slide shaft, so walkers can follow the slide down.
- **Shore path, B3.** Runs from the Geyser Plaza to the Wave Grotto beach.
- **River boat, B2.** Optional; a riverside boardwalk follows the same route.

## Water path
Spring (D2, Summit, 44 °C) → Caldera Crown → **two downhill branches**:
- **South branch:** Cascade Terraces down the south face → enters the mountain beside the arrival hall → mist corridors (B1, 34 °C) → underground river (B2) → Thunder Curtain Falls (B3) → Rimstone Terraces → Lazy River → Geyser Plaza → Wave Grotto (30 °C) → the sea.
- **North branch:** a separate trickle runs down the north cliff, filling each suite tub from the one above, and rejoins the river inside the mountain.

Water only ever flows downhill.

## Heights the plans don't give (suggested; refine them in planning)
- **Sea level: −70 m.** The B3 floor and the Wave Grotto beach sit at sea level. Ground (0 m) is a cliff-top 70 m above the sea, and B4 (−100 m) is below sea level.
- **Ceilings (suggested):**
  - Ground halls: about 8 m.
  - B1 caves: floor −20 m, ceilings about −8 to −6 m.
  - B2: river surface about −43 m, Kawagiri deck −40 m, ceiling about −28 m.
  - B3 cavern: floor −70 m, roof about −45 m. That makes Thunder Curtain Falls drop about 27 m, from the river lip to the plunge pool.
  - Leave at least 4–5 m of rock between one level's ceiling and the next level's floor.
- **Stair runs this implies:** at a 17 cm rise and 29 cm tread, a 20 m drop needs about 120 steps and about 35–45 m of run, including landings.
  - The Mist Steps (20 m) need to switch back or descend through a tunnel.
  - The Falls Stair (30 m) works best as switchbacks down the cliff.
  - The Lava Stair (40–50 m) spirals around the slide shaft.
  - The Spiral Stair at E3 spans 100 m, so it is mainly for exploring, and the lift carries most traffic.

## Known conflicts to resolve during planning (the builder decides)
- **The main lift at E3 overlaps the Obsidian Mirror (E2–F3) on B1 and Kawagiri (C2–F3) on B2.** Either shape the rooms around the lift core, or move the core to a nearby cell on every level. Whichever you choose, keep the lift vertical through all levels.
- **The Mist Steps (C3) sit next to or inside the Steam Grotto, and the Lava Stair (H4) sits next to the treatment rooms.** Shape around them.
- **The Terrace Path ends at E3, inside the Caldera Crown.** Move its top so it lands on the rim beside the pool.
- **Both ends of the cable lift are at E4.** Its top and bottom stations need different cells, or must be offset along the south slope.
- **The plans drift from the spec by about one cell** (see the brief), and some of them disagree with each other about the steam grotto and Kawagiri positions.
- **Kawagiri over the river or beside it:** the concept art (image 06) puts the dining pods directly over the river on beams, and that should win.
