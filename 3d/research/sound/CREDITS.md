### Processing
The processing is done by `tools/make_anchors.py`, with the specs in `.scratch/sound/anchor_spec.json`:
1. Mono at 32 kHz (24 kHz for surf).
2. Automatic choice of the best window: good loop match, no clicks, no wind rumble, and (for surf) the strongest wave surges.
3. High-pass at 25–60 Hz, and a high shelf of −3 to −5 dB above 5–6 kHz, so the procedural spray layer keeps the top octave.
4. Equal-power seam crossfade (1.5 s; 2 s for surf).
5. Normalised to −18 to −20 dBFS RMS, with peaks at −1 dBFS or lower.

Encoding is done by `tools/encode_bank.mjs`: the loop is wrapped with a 0.25 s pad, then encoded as Opus at 32 kbps (73–100 KB) and AAC at 48 kbps (127–175 KB).

The raw downloads stay in `.scratch/sound/cc0_r3/`, which is never shipped.
