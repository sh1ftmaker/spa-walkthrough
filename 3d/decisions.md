## Checks (class policies; thresholds unchanged)
- **Overlaps.** Assets flagged `cluster_ok` (rocks, boulders, stalactites, sea stacks, stepping stones) may touch each other, because a rock cluster interpenetrates by nature. Lights and small props (< 1 m²) may sit inside `host` assets (pavilions, pods, treatment rooms, the ramen counter, the kitchen). Every other overlap still needs an explicit `allow_overlap` in the space file.
- **Contact.** Probes start 0.35 m above the base and count only up-facing surfaces, so an edge rock that leans into a cave wall isn't reported as sunk. Floating is still > 2 cm everywhere.
- **The walk corridor is enforced by the engine.** `place.py` keeps every rule-placed item out of a 1.2 m corridor along each spine and branch, and out of doorway zones. Heroes and anchored pieces are deliberate and are only flagged by the checks.
- **Review rounds before moving on** (owner, 2026-10-26). The graybox and the material library each go through independent review rounds before detailed modelling: a fresh reviewer per round, fixes applied by the builders as rule or material changes, up to 3 rounds or every score ≥ 4. Generators are parked until the graybox passes.
- **Fix rounds (space agents → lead graybox changes).** The Mist Steps hall no longer carves through B1: it stays below −21.2, with a well only over flight 1. Corridor pools are 0.18 m deep. Rock now separates the grotto (south edge y 83.5) from the west corridor. The treatment doors reach the hall. The slide tube starts west at the launch gate. Suite rooms solidify outwards, so the floor meets the balcony. Summit paths are flat across their full width. Rock over the ground halls sits ≥ 1 m above their ceilings. The lower cascade decks are built at their stepped levels. The Wave Grotto beach slopes from −68 to the −70 waterline. The falls head is a railed balcony over the open east part of the falls shaft (to −32).
- **place.py side keywords.** `out`/`outer` and `in`/`inner` on along-rules mean away from or toward the polyline's centroid, so pool-rim rules work whatever the outline's winding. The slide shaft void stops at the B3 floor (it had dug a 0.5 m pit round the splash basin).
- **Graybox render hierarchy (review r1 feedback).** The hero glows and carries its own light, so it is the brightest thing. Lanterns glow softly. Railings render as thin dark proxies (no longer structural). Outdoor views get a moonlit sky and a lighter sea, so the surf and cliffs read.
- **Cliff Stair lanes moved to y 111.0 / 113.4**, clear of the suite balcony slabs (y ≤ 110). The inner lane passed under each balcony with < 2 m headroom, which cut the walk below every tier.
- **Graybox pipeline fixes from review r1:** rails are continuous runs; marker uplights are bright enough to read; ground snapping probes from 4 m up, so beaches and decks above the space floor are found; the slide-shaft lava glow is dimmed so it no longer outshines the heroes; the summit ground drops beyond the infinity lip.
- **Graybox hero rendering:** heroes that are light sources (lanterns, lava, neon, plume, ice, falls) glow in their own colour; other heroes are darker orange clay with a key light. The Mirror pool has no proxy, and its water surface is the hero for the visibility check.
- **Sound (research done).** Fully procedural, self-made bank (bubble-model water, wind, chimes, lava), portal-graph acoustics, 14 voices. The audio blocks merge into the space files after the graybox review rounds, so they don't collide with the agents' edits. Portal added between the koi junction and the Mirror corridor (found by the sound research), plus a B2-side portal at the Mist Steps foot.
- **Kawagiri floor is a sparse slatted hinoki deck** (owner, 2026-10-26), so the turquoise river rushes visibly about 2 m below. Boards are about 12 cm with about 6 cm gaps, laid across the walking direction on stringers. The river under the deck gets riverbed glow and mist so it reads through the gaps. Walk collision stays solid. Asset `deck_slatted`; generator and Low-tier budget to follow.
- **Sound direction (owner):** rushing water and waterfall thunder are the bed wherever water moves; bubbling and trickling become sparse close-range accents.
- **Water rendering (research done):** baked flow maps plus flow-aligned foam for rivers and rapids; free-fall-speed waterfall cards with stateless GPU particles for spray and mist; an analytic shoaling wave model shared by water, sand and spray in the Wave Grotto; a small ripple sim (128², 64² on Low) for hot pools; a planar mirror on High with a probe on Low. Estimated 2.3–4.8 ms on a phone at Low, with overdraw as the main cost. Cut order is in 3d/research/water/README.md. Note for the runtime: drop `powerPreference: 'high-performance'` (fails on ANGLE/Vulkan).
- **Water direction (owner, round 2):** rely on simulation rather than baking. Breakers must curl. Kawagiri curtains are laminar, glassy sheets. Falls must show no visible tiling. Spray must not look cheap: dense, lit and depth-soft. A WebGPU compute High tier is allowed; Low keeps a WebGL2 approximation of the same look.
- **Mist simulation (owner):** a cheap 2D stable-fluids solver runs in a Web Worker at about 20–30 Hz per zone. It feeds a density texture into the height-fog shader, with sources over warm water, falls jets, vents and portal drafts, and the player's wake. It's prototyped separately from the water agent's spray.
- **Sound: CC0 anchors approved (owner).** One licence-checked CC0 recording each, of about 100 KB in Opus, sits under Thunder Curtain, the B2 river and the sea-cave surf, credited in 3d/research/sound/CREDITS.md. Everything else stays procedural.
- **Cascade terraces step down with their pools:** inside g_cascade the ground takes the level of the nearest deck or pool rim. **Suite tier setbacks declined for now:** image 32 shows the tiers stacked vertically, and setbacks would mean re-solving the Cliff Stair (Should tier). Ledge cameras can frame the suites instead.
- **Ceilings follow their declared profiles** (dome, vault, tube, cave). The shell subdivides each ceiling and shapes it from the profile's min_z at the walls to the layout ceiling at the crown, with rock noise on caves. Glowworm spaces get a speckled cyan roof. - **Water round 2:** GPU simulations for the falls sheet (256×112, no tiling), river foam advection and pool ripples; curling breaker lips; a laminar refractive curtain; 24k lit droplets for spray. - **Materials as runtime shaders (owner):** most materials become web shaders: anti-tiled sampling (hex or stochastic blending, per-tile rotation), macro variation, distance-faded detail, triplanar on cave rock, wetness, and fully procedural variants where they beat textures. - **Asset list before modelling (owner):** a master list of 100–200 parametric generators from each location's references, with animation types. Concept sheets of about 6 assets per Codex image are shown to the owner before any modelling starts.
- **Mist (research done):** a 2D stable-fluids solver in a Web Worker (64² at 20 Hz on Low, 128² at 30 Hz on desktop High, one zone live at a time). It costs 0.01–0.05 ms per frame on the main thread. It drives the height-fog layer, with sources set by water temperature, drafts, bursts and the walker's wake. No SharedArrayBuffer. Phone GPU is estimated at about 1 ms on Low, to be confirmed on a device. The runtime patch is in 3d/research/mist/runtime-materials.patch.
