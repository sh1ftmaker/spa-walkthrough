The build agent records each conflict here, along with what it chose and why.

Limits: 1 GB site, 100 MB per file, so keep each part under about 50 MB.

## Plan (metric layout, `tools/plan/build_layout.py` → `3d/layout.json`)
- **Coordinates.** x east from the west edge of A, y north from the south edge of row 6, z up, metres. three.js uses (x, z, −y).
- **Main lift + Spiral Stair stay at E3 (90, 70) on every level** (spec), even though the plans draw it at E4 (B1) and E2 (B4). Rooms are shaped around it: the **Obsidian Mirror** is centred at (112, 92) (mostly F2, pool r 12, dome r 19) so the B1 lift lobby sits at its south-west. On B2 the lift sits in the Kawagiri core on the south bank. The spiral is a 588-step helix, walking radius 5.6 m, about 6.9 turns from 0 to −100, with doorways at each level.
- **Steam Grotto vs Mist Steps.** The grotto occupies x 40–80, y 82–112 (mostly C2/D2, reaching into row 1 as the plans draw it). The Mist Steps keep spec cell C3 as a 3-flight switchback (118 steps, 4 m flights) from B1 to the Kawagiri core.
- **Lava Stair vs treatment rooms.** The slide and Lava Stair shaft keep H4 (150, 50) on B1–B3. Treatment rooms take G3–H3 north of it (y 60–80). The Lava Stair is a 276-step helix around the glass slide shaft, with a door at B2.
- **Kawagiri over the river** (image 06 wins). The river widens to about 22 m under the restaurant. Both pod rows (T1–T6, T7–T12) stand on beams over the water on either side of a spine deck. The core (lift, kitchen, ramen counter, Mist Steps foot) sits on the south bank. The plan's south pod row over dry rock is not used.
- **B3 roof lowered to −50** (spec suggested about −45). The B2 river bed is at −44.6, and the spec value would leave only about 1 m of rock. The falls have their own shaft (x 8–46, y 40–80) open from −40 down to the pool.
- **B3 water heights so water flows downhill.** Thunder Curtain plunge pool −60.0 (falls drop 17 m from the −43 lip, not 27) → Rimstone −60.5 to −66.5 → Lazy River −67.45/−67.5 → Geyser pool −67.55 → splashdown −67.6 → sea −70.0. B3 main decks are at −67; the beach slopes to the sea. The spec's "B3 floor −70" is the sea and beach level.
- **Falls Stair.** It descends the east, south and west walls of the falls shaft, crosses a gallery **behind the curtain** at −55 (y 78.5), then drops to the pool deck at −59.5. The rimstone stair continues to −67.
- **Terrace Path moved to the west flank.** Drawn on the south-west slope (B5→E3), it would cross the gate court, the approach road and the arrival hall roof. It now climbs six north–south switchback legs on the west flank (x 6–46, y 70–116) from the gate court to the plateau at C3, then follows the rim walk to the pool, so its top is on the rim, not in the pool. The Cascade Terraces step down beside it and finish in the B5 pools as the ground plan shows (waterfalls at A4, pools at B5).
- **Cable lift ends.** The base is at E5 on the south terrace (88, 30) and the top station on the rim at E3 (96, 62). That is a steep inclined funicular up the cliff above the halls. It's an extra and doesn't count for walkability.
- **Arrival hall position.** It uses the spec cells C3–E4 (y 42–80). The ground painting draws it about 1.5 cells further north. The lava window is on the hall's north wall (x 44–78), clear of the lift core.
- **North cliff suites (provisional, Should tier).** The face is at y 106. Rooms are carved into y 94–106 with balconies to y 110, at tiers +10/+26/+43/+60, x 148/168/188. The Cliff Stair rises as stacked east–west ledge flights on the face west of the suites (x 124–140), from a ground ledge at the cliff foot to the moon deck. It will be refined when the suites are built.
- **Water levels.** B1 corridors, the Mirror and the grotto pools share one surface at −20.05, 5 cm below the −20.0 rims and stepping-stone tops. Corridor water leaves by the J2 outfall shaft to the river head (−42.0) and by the Mist Steps sheet. The north branch trickles through the suite tubs to the river head.
- **East terrace and the moon deck.** The moon deck (+120.5, H2) would have sat directly over the ground east terrace. The terrace is trimmed to y 42–78, and a sea-cliff path (g_east_path) runs round the NE shoulder to the north cliff foot ledge, where the Cliff Stair starts.

## Plan review (space-file subagents found these; applied to the layout)
- **Arrival hall vault raised from 9 m to 16 m**, to match the cave-hall scale of concept 04 (spec suggested about 8 m). The spiral now starts at the hall's south door (a_start 270°).
- **The river flows round the lift tower.** The B2 river footprint and the Kawagiri deck exclude the spiral drum (r 8.4–8.8). Before this, the water plane ran through the stair well, which the walk check caught.
- **Thunder Curtain.** The lip is 12 m wide (x 23–35, the river's width). The falls shaft's north edge follows the lip, so the shaft no longer runs open under the river. The walkway behind the curtain is its own carved gallery (b3_falls_gallery, −55, roof −52, 7 m of rock under the river bed). The plunge pool reaches y 77 to catch the curtain. A high view arch (x 46, y 49–73, −57 to −50) lets the curtain's glow read across the cavern.
- **B3.** A 32-step north-walk stair climbs from the lobby (−67) to the terrace shelf (−61.5). The splash pool moved to (154, 45), r 4.5, so the Lava Stair foot is dry. A portal joins the rimstone to the lazy river. The shore passage is low (roof −62.5) before the beach reveal. Six beach steps lead down to −68. The cavern's east wall follows the beach edge.
- **Summit rim walk off the infinity edge** (concepts 11 and 30). Walkers no longer stand on the pool's horizon. The rim reaches the pool from the south-west and the north, the moon deck by 3 risers, and the spring terrace by 9 risers. The Terrace Path head is its own space (s_rim_west).
- **Cliff Stair re-solved in five sections** that land exactly on the suite tiers (+10/+26/+43/+60, then +120), on separate flight lanes. Balconies bridge the gullies between suites.
- **The B1 culvert top is −4.2**, so the lowest cascade pool (−3.95) still drains downhill to it.
- **Kept as given:** portals that sit slightly inside footprint overlaps (boat landing, B4 tunnel). They are harmless.
- **Progress site gallery.** The owner asked (2026-10-26) for materials and models to appear on the progress site as they arrive. `tools/sync_gallery.py` posts every new material preview, LOD sheet and model render, paired with its concept image, and runs whenever an agent delivers.

## Checks (class policies; thresholds unchanged)
- **Overlaps.** Assets flagged `cluster_ok` (rocks, boulders, stalactites, sea stacks, stepping stones) may touch each other, because a rock cluster interpenetrates by nature. Lights and small props (< 1 m²) may sit inside `host` assets (pavilions, pods, treatment rooms, the ramen counter, the kitchen). Every other overlap still needs an explicit `allow_overlap` in the space file.
- **Contact.** Probes start 0.35 m above the base and count only up-facing surfaces, so an edge rock that leans into a cave wall isn't reported as sunk. Floating is still > 2 cm everywhere.
- **The walk corridor is enforced by the engine.** `place.py` keeps every rule-placed item out of a 1.2 m corridor along each spine and branch, and out of doorway zones. Heroes and anchored pieces are deliberate and are only flagged by the checks.
